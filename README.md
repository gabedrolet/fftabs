# fftabs: Foo Fighters tabs archive

**[fftabs.com](https://fftabs.com)**, also at [gabedrolet.github.io/fftabs](https://gabedrolet.github.io/fftabs/)

fftabs.com was a fan site collecting guitar, bass and drums tablatures and lyrics for the Foo Fighters, plus side projects and covers. This repository keeps that collection online as a free, static archive.

## What's in it

- **275 guitar, 152 bass and 71 drums tabs**, plus lyrics for 159 songs
- Every studio album from *Foo Fighters* (1995) to *But Here We Are* (2023), plus *Skin And Bones* and *Greatest Hits*
- B-sides, acoustic and alternate versions, soundtracks, studio and live covers
- Side projects: Probot, Jackson United, Taylor Hawkins & The Coattail Riders, Late! (Pocketwatch)

## Browsing

- **By album:** the home page shows every album. Open one to see its tracks, with buttons for guitar, bass, drums and lyrics.
- **By instrument:** the Guitar, Bass, Drums and Lyrics menus list every song that has that part, grouped by album.
- **Search:** type a song or album name in the search box, or press `/` to jump to it.
- **On a tab:** use A− / A+ to change the text size, and the ← → keys for the previous or next song. "Open raw file" shows the plain-text tab.

Links from the old fftabs.com pages forward to their new location.

## Corrections

Spotted a wrong tab or a typo in the lyrics? [Open an issue](https://github.com/gabedrolet/fftabs/issues) with the song and what should change.
